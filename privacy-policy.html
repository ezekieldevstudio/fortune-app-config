Fortune App

Privacy Policy

Fortune is under development, and its name is temporary. This policy describes the current app design and distinguishes planned features from features available now.

Purpose of the app

Fortune is intended for entertainment and self-reflection. Card readings do not guarantee predictions and are not professional advice. The current reading text is a sample, not an analysis generated from your chosen topic, mood, or card combination.

Information used in the current app

No account is currently required. As part of the current design, the app does not ask for your real name, birth date, gender, or free-form personal questions.

You can choose a topic, a mood, and cards from the available options. These selections are used to display your reading flow within the app.

Reading data and local storage

The current development version keeps your selections temporarily in app memory during a reading. It does not yet save reading history permanently. This temporary state may be lost when the app is fully closed or restarted.

Reading data is intended to be stored locally on your device when a history feature is introduced. Local history is still being designed; it is not currently an available storage feature. The current app does not provide account-based server storage for readings.

Third-party services and future changes

Advertising services, including AdMob, are not currently enabled in the app. Third-party services may later be used for advertising or technical operation.

This policy will be updated before those services are enabled to describe the services used, any information they handle, and any relevant choices. Planned features should not be understood as data collection already taking place.
