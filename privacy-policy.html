Privacy Policy

FO PICK is under development. This policy describes the current app and distinguishes planned features from features available now.

Purpose of the app

FO PICK is intended for entertainment and self-reflection. Card readings do not guarantee predictions and are not professional advice. Readings are composed on your device using the selected topic, mood, cards, and their order.

Information used in the current app

No account is currently required. As part of the current design, the app does not ask for your real name, birth date, gender, or free-form personal questions.

You can choose a topic, a mood, and cards from the available options. These selections are used to compose and display your reading within the app.

Reading data and local storage

Your selected topic, mood, cards and their order, reading result, and reading timestamp may be stored locally on your device. Reading history allows you to revisit previous readings, including their original basic and detailed text.

You can delete individual readings from their history details and delete all reading history from the app settings. Local reading history is not currently synchronized to a FO PICK server or account. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data may remove local records.

Usage-limit state is also stored locally to manage normal app usage, including which free reading periods have been used. It may include a reading/session identifier, usage day, reading type, consumption timestamp, and timezone offset. Usage records are separate from reading history; deleting reading history does not restore used reading allowances.

Access to detailed readings and earned bonus allowances is stored separately from the reading text. Local reward transaction identifiers and completion timestamps support duplicate prevention and retrying interrupted saves. Deleting a reading removes its detailed-reading access; it does not reset usage allowances. Pending reward receipts are removed after application, including when the associated reading has been deleted.

This local app storage is not encrypted secure storage and should not be used for confidential information. Unfinished reading selections may remain only in memory and may be lost when the app closes.

Optional rewarded advertising

FO PICK integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded advertising. Ads are available in native builds with the SDK installed, valid advertising configuration, and permission to request ads. Development testing uses Google's test ads; production advertising is not served without production configuration.

You choose whether to watch an ad to open the detailed interpretation of an existing reading, or to receive one extra Single or Triple reading after using all three regular reading periods for that usage day. Basic readings remain available without watching an ad, within the app's free usage limits. Ads do not change your cards or interpretation and do not grant extra FO PICK readings.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The information processed depends on the SDK, device settings, configuration, and applicable privacy choices. FO PICK does not request precise location for ads, or directly send your selected topic, mood, cards, or reading text as advertising targeting values.

After the consent check permits ad requests, the app may preload a rewarded ad before you choose to watch it. This can involve communication with Google's advertising services. No FO PICK account or server-side reading synchronization is required.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Consent and privacy choices

FO PICK uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and present Google's consent form where required. Ads are requested only when UMP indicates that they can be requested. Where UMP requires a privacy options entry point, the app's settings provide access to Google's privacy options form. Choices and their availability depend on your region and the configured messages. Relevant consent preferences may be stored by the SDK.
